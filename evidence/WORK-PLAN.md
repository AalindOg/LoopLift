# LoopLift — four work blocks and evidence packs

**Planning target:** 27 hours, split into 7 + 7 + 7 + 6. These are prospective time budgets, not recorded work. Four 7-hour sessions would total 28 hours.

Each pack will have seven distinct analysis images and an evidence-based entry draft. A draft describes only analysis actually executed; it does not certify the participant's time, independent authorship or a physical test. Record your real time and your actual contributions when posting. Do not post unused plans as completed sessions.

## Block 1 — feed and discharge (budget: 7 hours)

Study the current mechanism; trace bottom loading, retained pocket motion, top discharge and catch geometry. Evaluate exact sphere/solid clearances and inlet alignment. Distinguish a static collision-free path from self-feeding under gravity. Make a physical test checklist and choose the first interface revision after reviewing results.

Seven evidence subjects: assembly interfaces; inlet section; inlet sphere clearance; outlet section; outlet clearance; loading alignment window; tilt/gravity sensitivity.

## Block 2 — drive and tolerance engineering (budget: 7 hours)

Review the keyed shaft, bearing bores, axial stack, guard gaps, crank radius and tolerances. Sweep moving geometry against fixed geometry, quantify sensitivity to dimensional variation, and create an expanded coupon if justified. Work out assembly/retainer gaps instead of treating an attractive exploded render as proof of fastening.

Seven evidence subjects: axial stack; shaft-key cross section; bearing tolerance budget; crank radius sweep; pocket sensitivity; guard axial tolerance; proposed fit coupon geometry.

## Block 3 — gravity return (budget: 7 hours)

Audit floor heights, corner catch cups, return-to-funnel transition, downhill grade and the assumptions behind ideal rolling/ballistic calculations. Test candidate catch geometry when possible. Do not mistake ideal trajectories for a real dynamics simulation or physical test.

Seven evidence subjects: route plan; grade profile; first corner projection; second corner projection; catch capture envelope; funnel return interface; revised cup study.

## Block 4 — fabrication and release (budget: 6 hours)

Audit every part against exported topology, bed bounds, print orientation, slicer outputs, material/time estimates and the hardware list. Compare traceable source revisions and licences. Prioritise coupon and single-interface prints before the complete machine; actual print/testing requires the participant and equipment.

Seven evidence subjects: part inventory; bed fit; material breakdown; time breakdown; supports/orientation; verification coverage; physical test acceptance plan.

## Entry format

For each actual session: goal → work performed → concrete result or failure → decision → next step. Mention relevant image filenames, code/check outputs and AI assistance. Time fields stay blank until supplied from actual work. Images are technical evidence, not hour counters.
